# Employee-Engagement-Burnout-Analysis
Power BI dashboard analyzing employee engagement, satisfaction, workload stress, and burnout risk at Palo Alto Networks.

Employee Engagement, Satisfaction & Burnout Diagnostic Analysis
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee engagement, satisfaction, workload stress, and burnout risk using the Palo Alto Networks HR dataset. The objective is to help HR identify early warning signs of disengagement and burnout before employees decide to leave the organization.

🎯 Objectives
Build an Engagement Index using multiple satisfaction dimensions.
Identify employees at High, Medium, and Low Burnout Risk.
Analyze workload stress based on overtime and business travel.
Study engagement across departments, job roles, and career stages.
Provide actionable insights for HR managers through interactive dashboards.

📊 Key Insights
Employees working overtime showed higher burnout levels.
Lower work-life balance is associated with higher burnout risk.
Engagement varies across departments and job roles.
Long tenure in the same role may indicate stagnation.
High burnout employees can be identified before attrition occurs.
💼 Business Recommendations
Reduce excessive overtime.
Improve work-life balance initiatives.
Monitor departments with high burnout.
Increase employee engagement programs.
Provide career development opportunities for long-tenured employees.
